Eligibility Charters

Science & Technology (B.Tech / B.S.)

  • 10+2 with Physics, Mathematics & Chemistry/Computing from a recognized board.
  • Minimum 60% aggregate (55% for SC/ST/Maritime Quota).
  • Valid score in VIBE-SAT, JEE Main, or State Engineering CET.

Business & Leadership (B.B.A. / B.Com)

  • 10+2 in any stream (Commerce, Science, or Arts) with minimum 55% marks.
  • Demonstrated curiosity for enterprise, trade, or collaborative leadership.

Arts & Humanities (B.A. Hons)

  • 10+2 in any discipline with minimum 50% marks and fluency in English.
  • Submission of a creative portfolio, essay scroll, or historical query.
