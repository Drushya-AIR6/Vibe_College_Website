Eligibility

  • 10+2 or equivalent with minimum 50% aggregate
  • Physics, Chemistry, Maths for B.Tech programmes
  • Any stream for BBA and B.Com
  • Valid JEE / CUET / State CET score preferred
  • Direct admission on merit for ≥ 80% students

Document Checklist

  • 10th and 12th mark sheets (originals + 2 copies)
  • Transfer & Migration Certificate
  • Character Certificate from last institution
  • Passport-size photographs (6 copies)
  • ID proof (Aadhaar / Passport)

Scholarships

  • Gold Anchor: 100% tuition waiver — top 5 rankers
  • Silver Sail: 50% fee waiver — merit 80%+ in board exams
  • Crew Solidarity: 25% waiver — economically disadvantaged
  • Sports Compass: for state/national level athletes
  • Government scholarships honoured (SC/ST/OBC/EWS)

THE VIBE COLLEGE PIRATE CODE — ARTICLES OF HONOUR
I

Every sailor is equal on this ship.

No crew member shall be discriminated against on grounds of caste, creed, religion, gender or background. Ragging is a crime and will result in immediate dismissal.

II

Respect the ship and those who sail it.

College property, staff, fellow students and visitors shall be treated with dignity and care. Damage to property is grounds for disciplinary action.

III

Attendance keeps the ship afloat.

A minimum of 75% attendance is required in every subject. Academic honesty in examinations and assignments is non-negotiable.

IV

Use the web wisely.

College Wi-Fi and computing resources are for academic use. Misuse, hacking or harassment using digital means is a punishable offence.

V

Every voice matters — raise it right.

Grievances must be raised through the official Student Grievance Cell or Anti-Ragging Committee. Mob action or intimidation will not be tolerated.

SIGNED: THE ADMIRAL · DR. VIVEKA CHANDRA · PRINCIPAL
