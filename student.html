CADET KNOWLEDGE BASE · SHIP'S MANUAL

Frequently Asked Questions for the Crew.

Everything you need to know about navigating exams, rations, library scrolls, and hostel berths.

Cadets must maintain a minimum of 75% sea-readiness attendance across each voyage module to qualify for the end-semester exams and placement sea-trials. Cadets with medical leaves endorsed by the Chief Infirmary Officer receive a dispensation down to 65%.
Cabin re-allocations are processed during the first two weeks of each term. Submit a formal request to the Hostel Warden Office (Barracks Deck 1) or petition via the Signal Captain's HQ query channel below with reason (e.g. medical AC requirement).
Digital hall tickets are published 7 tide days before examinations directly under the Ship's Log Circulars tab on this portal. Ensure all doubloon semester fees show ₹0.00 clearance before downloading.
Special reserve manuscripts (like High Admiral Sterling's rare maritime law scrolls) can be consulted in the Rare Chart Room on Deck 1 with your physical Cadet ID. Reference photocopies can be requested at the RFID self-service desk.
Cadets seeking overnight shore leave must log their digital gate pass with their resident warden by 17:00 tide hours. The biometric scanner at the harbor gate logs departures and arrivals. Curfew for normal day outings is 22:00 hours.
Grade contestations must be filed within 10 days of transcript release using the File Grievance Scroll option in the Pirate Code charter or by submitting a formal appeal through your student query channel.
A crew of at least 15 cadets with an endorsing faculty navigator may petition the Dean of Student Affairs. Approved guilds receive up to ₹50,000 annual activity budget and hangar space.
The on-deck Infirmary is staffed 24 hours daily with an on-duty surgeon and resident nurse. Emergency ambulance transport to the mainland hospital is always docked at Pier 1. Emergency hotline: +1 800-555-8423.
